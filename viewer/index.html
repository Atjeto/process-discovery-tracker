      How to use
      
        
      
      
      
      
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
      
      
      
      

        
      
          
          
      This guide gets a process manager started with the Process Discovery Tracker (PDT). It follows one example: an order-to-cash process. The SME recorded four orders. For each order they found the customer in Salesforce, entered the order, created the sales order in SAP, updated the tracking sheet in Excel and sent the confirmation email from Outlook.
      
          
      What PDT does
      
          
        
            
      1. The SME records the process a few times: each pass is a transaction, for example one order.
        2. 
            
      2. PDT drafts the steps of each transaction and draws the process map.
        3. 
            
      3. You fix and name the steps of one transaction, the reference transaction, and confirm it.
        4. 
            
      4. You write the document fields, and PDT writes the PDD with the map.
        5. 
            
      5. You hand the PDD and the whole project to the developers as a numbered version.
        6. 
          
      
          
      Start a project
      
          
        
            
      • From the SME's recording. The SME records with the capture app and sends you the zip it makes (Make a zip for the analyst). Right-click the zip and choose Extract All. In PDT, choose Open, Open project... and pick the folder it made. PDT analyzes the recording by itself: the top bar says Updating steps and map... until it is done.
        • 
            
      • From a video. Choose New, New project from a video... and pick the video, for example a Teams recording of the SME at work. Then name the project: its folder is made where you save it, with a copy of the video. You add the steps as you watch.
        • 
            
      • From nothing. Choose New, New empty project... and name the project. You add the steps by hand.
        • 
          
      
          
      Help, Recording keys, lists what to tell the SME before they record.
      
          
      Your first hour
      
          
      The Overview's Checklist lists the main acts below and ticks each one when it is done; its button takes you to the next.
      
          
        
            
      1. Attach the video and the transcript. On the Overview, click Attach after No video and pick the video. PDT opens the Timeline: pause the video at a moment you recognize, click Mark frame, then click the same moment on the timeline. The video is copied into the project folder. Attach the meeting transcript (a Teams .vtt or .docx) with Attach after No transcript: the synced video places its sentences in time. Without a video, Sync on the Timeline's Transcript lane places it: pick a sentence, then click where it was said.
        2. 
            
      2. Check the transactions. On the Timeline, each transaction is a band, from Order #10900 to Order #10903. If one is missing, right-click the timeline where it starts, choose Start a transaction here..., then click Apply marker changes in the top bar.
        3. 
            
      3. Name the steps of the reference transaction, or pick them from the library. In Steps, transaction 1 is the reference transaction. Where the recorder cut a step wrongly, split or merge it first (see Everyday tasks). Then click each step and type its name in the step pane: the step that ends at Save order becomes Enter the order in Salesforce. The same drafted step in the other transactions takes the name too. Where the library has activities for the step's system, the Name field lists them: pick one instead of typing.
        4. 
            
      4. Write short descriptions. Description in the step pane is the text the PDD prints under the step. One or two sentences are enough, for example: Search the account by customer ID and open it. The grey text in an empty Description is what the recorder saw; the PDD does not print it. With a transcript, Said during this step lists what the SME said then, and Add copies a sentence into the description.
        5. 
            
      5. Set the flow where it branches or loops. Transaction 3 sent no confirmation email. On Send the confirmation email, click Only when... and type the customer asked for a confirmation. In transaction 2 the SME looked up a mapping in Notepad and did the SAP steps again. On Create the sales order in SAP, click Go to..., pick Check the sales order in SAP and type when: SAP rejects the material number. The map draws both.
        6. 
            
      6. Confirm the transaction. Click Confirm transaction (Ctrl+Enter). The steps you did not change are accepted as they are. Undo confirm, beside the Confirmed badge, takes it back.
        7. 
            
      7. Look at how the other transactions differ. Open transactions 2, 3 and 4 in Steps. Each lists how it differs from the reference transaction. Transaction 2 has an extra step (the Notepad lookup) and repeated steps. Transaction 3 reads Not done for the email, which is no difference once the email has its condition. Transaction 4 has no differences. The other transactions are not confirmed.
        8. 
            
      8. Fill the document fields on the Overview. Open Document and write each field: title, purpose, scope, trigger, result, key contacts, business rules, exceptions, and the improvement and automation scope (notes are optional). For example, the title Order to cash and the trigger A customer order arrives by email. The PDD prints each field in its own place, and the title names the files PDT makes: Order to cash PDD.docx. With a transcript, Add to puts a sentence into a field. The Observations under the document are measured from the recording (here, the product code is typed again in SAP as the material number), and the PDD prints them too.
        9. 
            
      9. Check the Map. The Map shows the map the PDD prints: a box per step in its system's colour, and the decisions from the flow you set. Click a box to see each time the step happened, with its moment in the video and its screenshots. Show how the transactions ran shows the paths the transactions took; point at a line to see how often.
        10. 
            
      10. Export the PDD. Choose Export, PDD (.docx) and save it. Its folder opens with the file selected. The PDD is written again from the project each time, so export again after a change.
        11. 
          
      
          
      Everyday tasks
      
          
      Add a step the recorder missed
      
          
        
            
      1. In Steps, select the step it comes after.
        2. 
            
      2. Click + Add step (or press N), type its name and click Add step.
        3. 
            
      3. In the step pane, set its System. Alt+Up and Alt+Down move it.
        4. 
            
      4. With a video, you can instead pause the video at that moment on the Timeline and click + Step (or press S): the step gets that time.
        5. 
          
      
          
      Split or merge steps
      
          
        
            
      1. Select the step in Steps and open More in the step pane.
        2. 
            
      2. To split it, click the scissors after the recorded action where the first part ends.
        3. 
            
      3. To merge it with the next step, click Merge with next. To merge several, tick them in the list and click Merge selected.
        4. 
            
      4. A merge is made in the other transactions too, but a name you then give the merged step is not: give it the same name in each transaction, so the map keeps one box.
        5. 
          
      
          
      Add a screenshot, a note or a file
      
          
        
            
      1. Select the step in Steps.
        2. 
            
      2. Add screenshot: a frame of the video, a pasted image (Ctrl+V) or an image file. Use in PDD picks the one the PDD prints under the step.
        3. 
            
      3. Add note: notes stay in the app, beside the step on the Map; the PDD does not print them.
        4. 
            
      4. Attach file...: for example the input data set or an example email. It is copied into the project folder, and the PDD lists it. For a file of the whole project, use Add file... under the document fields on the Overview.
        5. 
          
      
          
      Leave out an app or a stretch of the recording
      
          
        
            
      1. An app, for example Teams during the call: in Steps, click Apps outside the process..., tick the app and click Save.
        2. 
            
      2. A stretch, for example a break: on the Timeline, hold Shift and drag across it, click Leave out of the analysis, then Leave out.
        3. 
            
      3. The steps and the map are drafted again without it. A stretch left out shows hatched: click it and choose Include again to bring it back.
        4. 
          
      
          
      Make a sub-process
      
          
        
            
      1. To give a step its own steps, select it and click Open as a sub-process: Enter the order in Salesforce gets one step per field and click, from Enter Product code to Click Save order.
        2. 
            
      2. To group steps, tick steps that follow one another, click Group into a sub-process... and name it, for example Book the order in SAP.
        3. 
            
      3. Click ▸ 6 sub-steps on its row to open it; the line above the list goes back up. Dissolve sub-process undoes it.
        4. 
            
      4. In the PDD, each sub-process gets a chapter with its own map.
        5. 
          
      
          
      Edit the map as your As-Is map, and make a To-Be copy
      
          
        
            
      1. On the Map, click Edit as the As-Is map. The editor opens on a copy of the process map. Change it: the copy is kept once you change something.
        2. 
            
      2. Close the editor. The PDD now prints your As-Is map in place of the process map, and an analysis never changes it.
        3. 
            
      3. For the To-Be map, click Edit a new copy, set Use in PDD as to To-Be map and change the copy. The PDD prints it under the improvement scope.
        4. 
            
      4. Edited maps lists your copies, with Edit, Duplicate and Delete.
        5. 
          
      
          
      Open a second project in a new window
      
          
        
            
      1. Choose Open in a new window... in the Open menu, and pick the project folder.
        2. 
            
      2. It opens in a window of its own, and the project you had open stays open.
        3. 
            
      3. A project opens for editing in one window only: in a second window it opens read only.
        4. 
          
      
          
      Undo
      
          
        
            
      1. Press Ctrl+Z to undo your last change, and Ctrl+Y to redo it.
        2. 
            
      2. Undo covers every change (steps, markers, document fields, edited maps, the video, a stretch left out, a removed transcript), up to 50, until you open another project.
        3. 
            
      3. In the map editor, Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y undo and redo the changes to the map.
        4. 
          
      
          
      Hand over to the developers
      
          
        
            
      1. Choose Hand off... in the Export menu.
        2. 
            
      2. Type what changed in this version (for version 1 it says First hand-off already) and click Hand off.
        3. 
            
      3. PDT makes Order to cash v1.zip next to the project folder and opens its folder. Send that zip to the developers.
        4. 
          
      
          
      What the zip holds. The whole project folder, and at its top the PDD and the Technical details of that version: Order to cash v1 PDD.docx and Order to cash v1 Technical details.xlsx. The Technical details is a spreadsheet for the developers: every click and entry of the reference transaction with the control it acted on, the fields typed in each transaction, and the applications.
      
          
      How a developer opens it. In PDT, choose Open, Open a hand-off (.zip)... and pick the zip. It opens read only: everything can be looked at and played, and nothing can change.
      
          
      The next version. Keep working in your project, then choose Hand off... again: PDT makes Order to cash v2.zip. The PDD says which version it is and lists every version with what changed. Ctrl+Z takes a hand-off back; its zip stays.
      
          
      Where things are kept
      
          
        
            
      • The project folder holds everything: the recording, your work, the video, the transcript, screenshots, attached files, the library and your edited maps. Copy it whole, never in parts.
        • 
            
      • Every change you make saves itself to the project folder. The top bar says Saved; point at it to see the folder. If it says Not saved, point at it to see why.
        • 
            
      • Made on export: the PDD, the map (as an image or as BPMN) and the Technical details go where you save them. Whole project (.zip) and Hand off... make their zip next to the project folder. Every export opens its folder with the file selected.
        • 
            
      • Handing it on. To hand the project to someone, use Export, Whole project (.zip): one zip of the folder, made next to it. For the developers, use Hand off... instead.
        • 
          
      
          
      The library
      
          
      The library lists named activities for each system: for SAP, for example, Create sales order. In the step pane, click the Name field: it lists the activities of the step's system, and a pick gives the step its name, its system and, when you wrote none, its description. You can still type any name. Where the library has no activities for the system yet, the field says so. A project keeps the library it started with, so a newer library does not change it. The owner of PDT maintains the library, and it comes with the app.
      
          
      When something looks wrong
      
          
      
            
      "This folder is not a project: it has no manifest.json."
      
            
      The folder holds no recording. Unzip the SME's zip first (right-click it, Extract All) and open the folder it makes.
      
            
      "This recording has no transactions marked."
      
            
      The SME did not press the transaction key. On the Timeline, right-click where each transaction starts, choose Start a transaction here..., then click Apply marker changes.
      
            
      "The analysis failed, so the steps and the map are not drafted."
      
            
      The log at the top says why. Choose Help, Troubleshooting, Re-analyze: your confirmed, edited, added and hidden steps are kept.
      
            
      "The As-Is map was made before the steps changed: check it, or Copy again."
      
            
      On the Map, edit your As-Is map to match the steps, or click Copy again for a new copy of the process map as it is now.
      
          
      
          
      Glossary
      
          
      
            
      ProjectWhat you open or start in PDT: one folder with the recording or video, your work and all you add.
      
            
      RequestThe name of what the SME is asked to record, set in the capture app's Recording setup. The Overview shows it on the project line.
      
            
      SMEThe subject matter expert who does the process and records it.
      
            
      TransactionOne pass through the process, for example one order.
      
            
      StepOne piece of work in a transaction, for example Create the sales order in SAP.
      
            
      Reference transactionThe transaction you name, fix and confirm; the PDD is built around it.
      
            
      Sub-processA step with steps of its own.
      
            
      Process mapThe map PDT draws from the steps.
      
            
      As-Is mapYour edited copy of the process map; the PDD prints it in its place.
      
            
      To-Be mapA copy you change to show the process as it should be.
      
            
      PDDThe Process Definition Document: the Word document PDT writes.
      
            
      Technical detailsThe spreadsheet for the developers that comes with a hand-off.
      
            
      Hand-offA numbered version for the developers: one zip.
      
            
      LibraryThe named activities per system that a step's Name field lists.
      
          
      
        
      

        
      
          
      Transactions and drafted steps
      
          
      A transaction is one pass through the process, from a start marker to its end marker. The app drafts every transaction's steps: a step is one visit to a screen, and it ends at the action that commits the work there (Save, Search, Submit, Send) or where the work moves to another screen. Steps lists the transactions in time order; open one to see its steps. Steps outside every transaction are listed under Outside the transactions.
      
          
      Confirm the reference transaction
      
          
        
            
      1. Open the reference transaction: transaction 1, marked Reference, unless you choose another one (right-click a transaction, Use as reference transaction).
        2. 
            
      2. Check its steps against the screenshots or the video. Where a boundary is wrong, merge, split, add or hide steps (right-click a step, or use the step pane).
        3. 
            
      3. Name the steps the way the document should: double-click a name, or select the step and press Enter. In the step pane the name is saved when you press Enter or leave the field, the description when you leave it; there is no Save button, and Ctrl+Z undoes each change. A step's description is printed with it in the PDD.
        4. 
            
      4. Click Confirm transaction: the steps you did not change are accepted as they are. Undo confirm, beside the Confirmed badge, turns them back into drafts.
        5. 
          
      
          
      The step panel
      
          
      Select a step in Steps, click a box on the Map or a step band on the Timeline: the step panel opens beside it. It shows every time the step happens in the transactions, each with its moment (▶ plays it in the panel's own video), its screenshots, what was said and its notes. Watch in the step pane plays the step there.
      
          
      Fixes that carry to the other transactions
      
          
      A name you give a drafted step goes at once to the same drafted steps in the other transactions (each transaction says in one line how many of its names came that way). A merge in the reference transaction is also made in the other transactions wherever they have the same drafted steps one after the other, and one Ctrl+Z undoes all of it. Hiding a drafted step offers to hide it in every transaction. Splits and added steps are made in each transaction that needs them.
      
          
      Differences between transactions
      
          
      Every other transaction is compared with the reference transaction step by step, the way the PDD compares them: an open transaction lists its extra steps (and where they happen), its skipped steps, the steps done in a different order and the steps done more often than in the reference transaction, and its line counts them. Said apart, above them: when part of it was left out of the analysis, which part, and a step that happens only under a condition (Only when) and was not done, which is no difference. The other transactions are not confirmed: once the reference transaction is confirmed and each other one has been opened, the Overview's Checklist ticks Review the other transactions.
      
          
      Sub-processes
      
          
      A step can hold steps of its own: a sub-process. Open as a sub-process, in the step pane, gives a step one step per action recorded in it (a field typed, a click); Group into a sub-process, in the Steps toolbar once two or more steps one after the other are ticked, moves them into a new one. Either is made in every transaction. The ▸ N sub-steps button on its row opens it, one block per transaction that goes through it, and the line above the list goes back up. Dissolve sub-process undoes it, and Ctrl+Z undoes that. Confirm transaction confirms every level.
      
          
      The flow of the steps
      
          
      After a step the next one follows. Where the process branches or loops, say so in the step pane's Flow line: Only when gives the step a condition (the map draws a decision, with an "otherwise" path around it); Go to sends the process back to an earlier step, on to a later one or to its end, when a condition holds. Inside a sub-process the flow stays inside it: its end ends the sub-process.
      
          
      The activity library
      
          
      The step pane's Name offers the activities of the step's system from the activity library: pick one and the step takes its name and its system, and its description when you wrote none. You can still type any name. A project keeps the library it started with: its first analysis copies the app's library into the project folder as library.json. The app's library is the file library\activities.json in the app's resources folder; Build the library makes a new one from the names given in projects and hand-offs.
      
          
      
          
      What the step labels mean
      
          
      A step shows its label only where it differs from its transaction: the reference transaction's status (Draft or Confirmed) says it for the others, and a drafted step of another transaction shows none. Steps outside every transaction show theirs.
      
          
        
            
      • Draft drafted by the app and not checked yet; every analysis drafts it again.
        • 
            
      • Confirmed you accepted the draft as it is.
        • 
            
      • Edited you changed its name, its boundaries or its flags.
        • 
            
      • Added you added it by hand.
        • 
            
      • Hidden you took it out; Show hidden steps on its transaction lists it again.
        • 
          
      
          
      Every analysis keeps confirmed, edited, added and hidden steps as they are.
      
          
      Leaving things out
      
          
        
            
      • Apps outside the process, in the Steps toolbar: tick the apps that are not part of the process, for example Teams or Outlook used during the call; the steps and the map are updated without them.
        • 
            
      • Leave out of the analysis, on the Timeline: Shift-drag across a stretch (a break, a detour) and choose it on the card that opens. The steps and the map are drafted again without that stretch, and its time is not counted; a transaction with a stretch left out says so, and the reference transaction's steps done in it are not read as skipped. The Timeline shows the stretch hatched, Left out of the analysis: click it and choose Include again to bring it back. Ctrl+Z undoes either.
        • 
            
      • Hide step: takes one step out, and no analysis brings it back. Only a step you added can be deleted.
        • 
          
      
          
      Waste
      
          
      Flag a step as Waste when it should not be needed (rework, duplicate effort). The PDD prints the flag with the step, and the maps mark it: a dashed box on how the transactions ran, a Waste note beside its task on the process map. Branches and exceptions are not flags: the map and the differences between the transactions show them.
      
        
      

        
      
          
      What the Map shows
      
          
      The Map shows the map the PDD prints: your As-Is map when you made one, else the process map the app draws from the reference transaction and the others, a box per step in its system's colour, with its notes and files beside it. The line above the map says which one shows. Show how the transactions ran draws the map from the transactions as they ran: hover a line for how often it was taken and its time; a dashed line is a path set in Steps that no transaction took. Click a box for the step panel. Fit fits the map in view, and Reset layout puts back the boxes you dragged.
      
          
      Sub-processes
      
          
      When a step holds a sub-process, Map: above the map chooses the main process or a sub-process; its number is its chapter in the PDD, and Steps shows the same level. On the process map, the arrow on a sub-process opens its map; on how the transactions ran, a double-click on a box marked + does.
      
          
      Your As-Is and To-Be maps
      
          
        
            
      • Edit as the As-Is map copies the process map into the editor, and the PDD prints the copy in its place. Change it there (Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y undo and redo in the editor); it is kept once you change something, and analyzing the project again never changes it. When the steps change after it was made, the Map says so, with Copy again.
        • 
            
      • Edit a new copy, once the As-Is map exists, starts another copy: choose To-Be map in Use in PDD as, and the PDD prints it under its improvement scope.
        • 
            
      • Edited maps lists your copies: Edit, Use in PDD as (None, As-Is map, To-Be map), Duplicate, Copy again and Delete (Ctrl+Z brings a copy back).
        • 
          
      
          
      Exporting the map
      
          
      Export, Process map (image) saves the map the Map shows as an SVG image, for Figma, Lucidchart or a browser. Process map (BPMN) saves it as a BPMN 2.0 file, with every sub-process, for ADONIS, Bizagi or Camunda. Both keep the steps' notes, which the PDD's pictures leave out.
      
        
      

        
      
          
      Working with markers
      
          
      A transaction starts at a start marker and ends at its end marker; the SME places them with the recording bar or the recording keys. An edited marker changes the transactions once you apply it; the recording itself never changes.
      
          
        
            
      • Add: right-click the timeline at that moment and choose Start a transaction here or End the transaction here.
        • 
            
      • Rename, move or delete: in the Timeline's Markers panel. Double-click a Start marker to rename it.
        • 
            
      • Apply: a changed marker shows at once as pending. Apply marker changes, in the bar at the top, updates the transactions, the steps and the map.
        • 
          
      
          
      To join two transactions, delete the End of the first and the Start of the second. To split a transaction, start a transaction where its second part begins: a Start ends the transaction before it.
      
        
      

        
      
          
      What to tell the SME
      
          
      The SME records with the capture app on their own computer. These keys mark the transactions while recording:
      
          
      
            
      Ctrl+Shift+SThe transaction key: start the next transaction (Transaction 1, Transaction 2, and so on). Ends the previous transaction first, and starts the recording if none is running.
      
            
      Ctrl+Shift+EEnd the current transaction.
      
            
      Ctrl+Shift+PTake a screenshot by hand.
      
          
      
          
      The recording bar on the SME's screen does the same without the keys: Start recording, then Next transaction for each transaction, Pause and Stop. End transaction is in the capture app's tray menu.
      
        
      

        
        
      
          
      Handing a version to the developers
      
          
      Each hand-off gets the next number, and the PDD says which version it is: its Document control reads Version 2, handed off with the date and your name, over a table of every version and what changed. A PDD exported between hand-offs reads Working copy after version 2.
      
          
      Opening a hand-off
      
          
      Open a hand-off (.zip) is also a button on the page shown while no project is open. The app keeps its own copy of the hand-off. A hand-off unzipped by hand opens read only too. The Overview's project line reads the version, the date and who handed it off; Export opens the PDD and the Technical details the zip came with. A newer version is a zip of its own, opened the same way.
      
          
      The Technical details
      
          
      A spreadsheet of what the recording holds for a developer: every click, entry and choice of the reference transaction with the control it acted on and how the recorder identified it, every field with what was entered in each transaction (a password was never recorded), the values typed again in another system, and the programs. Export, Technical details (.xlsx) writes it on its own too.
      
        
      

        
      
          
      Keyboard shortcuts
      
          
      
            
      SpacePlay or pause the video
      
            
      ← →Move the video 5 seconds (hold Shift for 10)
      
            
      + − 0Zoom the timeline in, out, or back to the whole recording

      • Click a band on the timeline, or a marker in the Markers panel, to go to that moment. Click a screenshot to see it full size.
        • 
            
      • Click a step band on the timeline to open the step panel beside it.
        • 
            
      • Right-click the timeline to start or end a transaction there, or to add a note at that moment. Click a hatched stretch, left out of the analysis, to include it again.

      Common issues
      
          
      
            
      A recording key did nothing
      
            
      Another program on the SME's computer may hold the same key. The capture app says so when it starts: Keys changed names the key it uses instead, Keys not available means there is none. Use the recording bar (Start recording, then Next transaction) or the tray menu (Next transaction, End transaction), or close that program and start the capture app again.
      

            
      A Citrix or remote desktop window shows as one app
      
            
      The recorder cannot see inside a remote window from the SME's computer, so the work done in it shows as one app with no steps inside. Run the capture app inside the remote session, or rely on the transaction markers and a video for that part and add its steps by hand in Steps.
      

            
      The annotations for this project cannot be read
      
            
      A banner at the top says so, and nothing is saved while it shows. Restore the newest backup, on the banner, puts back the newest backup that can be read (it asks first and says when that backup was saved) and keeps the file that cannot be read beside it, as annotations.unreadable with the time.
      

            
      The steps or the map are missing or out of date
      
            
      The app analyzes a project by itself when it opens. If that stopped (it was cancelled, or it failed and the log at the top says why), analyze it again. Your confirmed, edited, added and hidden steps are kept.
