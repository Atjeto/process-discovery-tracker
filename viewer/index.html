      How to use
      
        
      
      
      
      
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
      
      
      
      

        
      
          
      What is a transaction?
      
          
      A transaction is one pass through the process: one order entered, one request handled. The SME records the process a few times and presses the transaction key (or the recording bar's button) at the start of each transaction. A project made from a video, or an empty one, is one transaction until you mark where each one starts on the Timeline.
      
          
      From a recording to the PDD
      
          
      The SME sends you the recording folder: open it with Open project. You confirm the first transaction's drafted steps, review how the other transactions differ, write the document and export the PDD. The Overview's Checklist ticks what is done, with one button for the next act.
      
          
        
            
      1. Transactions. Open the project: the app analyzes the recording by itself and drafts each transaction's steps and the map (the bar at the top says so while it works). Each transaction starts at a start marker; if one is missing, right-click the Timeline at that moment, choose Start a transaction here and click Apply marker changes.
        2. 
            
      2. Reference transaction. In Steps, check transaction 1's drafted steps against the screenshots or the video. Fix the boundaries (merge, split, add, hide), name the steps the way the document should, write a description where a step needs one, then click Confirm transaction.
        3. 
            
      3. Other transactions. The other transactions arrive drafted and named like the reference transaction. Open each one in Steps: it lists how it differs from the reference transaction (extra, skipped or reordered steps). Fix a step where needed; the other transactions are not confirmed.
        4. 
            
      4. Document. On the Overview, you write the document fields: title, purpose, scope, trigger, result, contacts, business rules, exceptions and the improvement and automation scope. The PDD prints them in place of its placeholders.
        5. 
            
      5. PDD. Export the PDD (.docx); every export opens its folder with the file selected. Its process map is your edited As-Is map when you made one (on the Map, Edit as the As-Is map), else the map from the transactions. Save as this process, on the confirmed reference transaction, keeps its steps on this computer: a later recording of the same process is compared with them.
        6. 
          
      
          
      Every change you make saves itself to the project folder: the top bar says Saved, and pointing at it shows the folder. That folder holds the whole project, with its video, transcript, screenshots, attachments and edited maps. To hand the project to someone, use Export, Whole project (.zip): one zip of the folder, made next to it.
      
          
      What the Overview shows
      
          
        
            
      • Checklist: the five acts above, each ticked once it is done. The export is never ticked: the app keeps no record of it.
        • 
            
      • Document: you write these fields; the PDD prints them. Its line names the fields still empty.
        • 
            
      • From the transcript: what was said outside the transactions, from the meeting transcript you attached; the app places each sentence by its time.
        • 
            
      • Observations: the app measures these from the recording: the data typed twice, and where the time goes (the average time of each step and system, and in how many transactions it happened).
        • 
          
      
          
      Optional inputs
      
          
        
            
      • A video of the recording, for example a Teams recording: attach it on the Overview (the line under the Checklist) or on the Timeline's video band, to check the steps against it and hear the SME explain. You sync it as you attach it: pause it at a moment you recognize, click Mark frame, then click the same moment on the timeline. It is copied into the project folder, so the folder carries it.
        • 
            
      • A meeting transcript (.vtt or .docx): attach it on the Overview (the line under the Checklist). Its sentences show beside the document fields, and with each step once the transcript is placed in time: a synced video places it, or use Sync on the Timeline's Transcript lane (pick a sentence, then click where it was said). A sentence said before the recording started shows how long before (for example 0:28 before). A sentence you add to a step's description or a field is printed in the PDD.
        • 
          
      
          
      Starting without a recording
      
          
      In the New menu, New project from a video makes a project from a video of the process (a Teams recording, a screen video): the video is copied into the new folder and the Timeline follows it. New empty project makes a project with nothing in it. Either is one transaction until you mark where each one starts on the Timeline. Add its steps in Steps with + Add step (Alt+Up and Alt+Down move a step without a time), or on the Timeline with + Step at the video's playhead. The step pane's System field says which system a step happens in.
      
          
      Screenshots, notes and attachments
      
          
      In the step pane, Add screenshot takes a frame of the video, a pasted image (Ctrl+V in the pane) or an image file. Use in PDD picks the one the PDD prints under the step; Remove takes one off (a recorded screenshot stays in the recording). A note belongs to a step (the step pane) or to a moment (right-click the Timeline, Add note here). The PDD's text leaves notes out, but its process map picture shows a step's notes beside the step (an edited As-Is map shows the notes it was copied with). Attach file in the step pane and Add file under the Overview's document fields copy a file (an example email, a spreadsheet, an SOP) into the project folder, and the PDD lists them. Ctrl+Z undoes each of these.
      
        
      

        
      
          
      Transactions and drafted steps
      
          
      A transaction is one pass through the process, from a start marker to its end marker. The app drafts every transaction's steps: a step is one visit to a screen, and it ends at the action that commits the work there (Save, Search, Submit, Send) or where the work moves to another screen. Steps lists the transactions in time order; open one to see its steps. Steps outside every transaction are listed under Outside the transactions.
      
          
      Confirm the reference transaction
      
          
        
            
      1. Open the reference transaction: transaction 1, marked Reference, unless you choose another one (right-click a transaction, Use as reference transaction).
        2. 
            
      2. Check its steps against the screenshots or the video. Where a boundary is wrong, merge, split, add or hide steps (right-click a step, or use the step pane).
        3. 
            
      3. Name the steps the way the document should: double-click a name, or select the step and press Enter. In the step pane the name is saved when you press Enter or leave the field, the description when you leave it; there is no Save button, and Ctrl+Z undoes each change. A step's description is printed with it in the PDD.
        4. 
            
      4. Click Confirm transaction: the steps you did not change are accepted as they are. Reopen transaction takes the confirmation back.
        5. 
          
      
          
      The step panel
      
          
      Select a step in Steps, click a box on the Map or a step band on the Timeline: the step panel opens beside it. It shows every time the step happens in the transactions, each with its moment (▶ plays it in the panel's own video), its screenshots, what was said and its notes. Watch in the step pane plays the step there.
      
          
      Fixes that carry to the other transactions
      
          
      A merge in the reference transaction is also made in the other transactions wherever they have the same drafted steps one after the other, and one Ctrl+Z undoes all of it. Hiding a drafted step offers to hide it in every transaction. Splits and added steps are made in each transaction that needs them.
      
          
      Differences between transactions
      
          
      Every other transaction is compared with the reference transaction step by step, the way the PDD compares them: an open transaction lists its extra steps (and where they happen), its skipped steps and the steps done in a different order. The other transactions are not confirmed: once each has been opened, the Overview's Checklist ticks Review the other transactions.
      
          
      Saved names
      
          
      A name you give a drafted step goes at once to the same drafted steps in the other transactions, and this computer saves it: the next analysis of this project or a later one drafts those steps with it (each transaction says in one line how many of its names came from saved names). Saved names, in the Steps toolbar, lists them: rename one, or forget it.
      
          
      Saved processes
      
          
      Once the reference transaction is confirmed, Save as this process keeps its steps on this computer. A later project of the same process is compared with the saved process until its own reference transaction is confirmed.
      
          
      What the step labels mean
      
          
      A step shows its label only where it differs from its transaction: the reference transaction's status (Draft or Confirmed) says it for the others, and a drafted step of another transaction shows none. Steps outside every transaction show theirs.
      
          
        
            
      • Draft drafted by the app and not checked yet; every analysis drafts it again.
        • 
            
      • Confirmed you accepted the draft as it is.
        • 
            
      • Edited you changed its name, its boundaries or its flags.
        • 
            
      • Added you added it by hand.
        • 
            
      • Hidden you took it out; Show hidden steps on its transaction lists it again.
        • 
          
      
          
      Every analysis keeps confirmed, edited, added and hidden steps as they are.
      
          
      Leaving things out
      
          
        
            
      • Leave apps out, in the Steps toolbar: tick the apps that are not part of the process; the steps and the map are updated without them.
        • 
            
      • Hide step: takes one step out, and no analysis brings it back. Only a step you added can be deleted.
        • 
          
      
          
      Waste
      
          
      Flag a step as Waste when it should not be needed (rework, duplicate effort). The PDD prints the flag with the step. Branches and exceptions are not flags: the map and the differences between the transactions show them.
      
        
      

        
      
          
      Working with markers
      
          
      A transaction starts at a start marker and ends at its end marker; the SME places them with the recording bar or the recording keys. An edited marker changes the transactions once you apply it; the recording itself never changes.
      
          
        
            
      • Add: right-click the timeline at that moment and choose Start a transaction here or End the transaction here.
        • 
            
      • Rename, move or delete: in the Timeline's Markers panel. Double-click a Start marker to rename it.
        • 
            
      • Apply: a changed marker shows at once as pending. Apply marker changes, in the bar at the top, updates the transactions, the steps and the map.
        • 
          
      
          
      To join two transactions, delete the End of the first and the Start of the second. To split a transaction, start a transaction where its second part begins: a Start ends the transaction before it.
      
        
      

        
      
          
      What to tell the SME
      
          
      The SME records with the capture app on their own computer. These keys mark the transactions while recording:
      
          
      
            
      Ctrl+Shift+SThe transaction key: start the next transaction (Transaction 1, Transaction 2, and so on). Ends the previous transaction first, and starts the recording if none is running.
      
            
      Ctrl+Shift+EEnd the current transaction.
      
            
      Ctrl+Shift+PTake a screenshot by hand.
      
          
      
          
      The recording bar on the SME's screen does the same without the keys: Start recording, then Next transaction for each transaction, Pause and Stop. End transaction is in the capture app's tray menu.

      Clicks
      
          
        
            
      • Click a band on the timeline, or a marker in the Markers panel, to go to that moment. Click a screenshot to see it full size.
        • 
            
      • Click a step band on the timeline to open the step panel beside it.
        • 
            
      • Right-click the timeline to start or end a transaction there, or to add a note at that moment.
        • 
            
      • While the steps and the map are updated, the bar at the top says so, with Cancel.

      Common issues
      
          
      
            
      A recording key did nothing
      
            
      Another program on the SME's computer may hold the same key. The capture app says so when it starts: Keys changed names the key it uses instead, Keys not available means there is none. Use the recording bar (Start recording, then Next transaction) or the tray menu (Next transaction, End transaction), or close that program and start the capture app again.
      

            
      A Citrix or remote desktop window shows as one app
      
            
      The recorder cannot see inside a remote window from the SME's computer, so the work done in it shows as one app with no steps inside. Run the capture app inside the remote session, or rely on the transaction markers and a video for that part and add its steps by hand in Steps.
      

            
      The steps or the map are missing or out of date
      
            
      The app analyzes a project by itself when it opens. If that stopped (it was cancelled, or it failed and the log at the top says why), analyze it again. Your confirmed, edited, added and hidden steps are kept.
