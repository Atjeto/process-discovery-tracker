Try it, in the order of the work

  1. Find where the invoice is discussed: open Transcript, type "invoice". The matches also light up on the timeline.
  2. Click a sentence. The screen and the playhead go there.
  3. Mark the price list detour as not part of the process: hold Shift and drag from 15:10 to 41:30 on the timeline (or press I and O at the two moments), then choose the button of that name. Play across it.
  4. Go to 53:20 and click Starts here on "File the order form in the shared folder". It keeps its place in the list.
  5. Click Add step (or press S) while it plays: the step is added and the call keeps playing. Pause and do it again: its name is ready to type.
  6. Click a step on the timeline, write its description, and add a sentence that was said during it.

What is different from today

  • The timeline keeps the full width, always. Nothing opens over it or beside it.
  • One screen, one set of play controls, on the timeline where the playhead is.
  • One pane with a tab for each kind of thing: the steps in the order of the process (also those without a time), the transcript, the screenshots, the notes, the attached files. A click on a lane opens its tab at that item, and a click in a tab goes to its moment. A step opens in place.
  • The Steps lane is the tall one. Step names are readable; a draft is dashed, an open step fades out.
  • A stretch that is not part of the process is hatched through every lane, skipped when playing, and left out of the transcript. Include again brings it back.
  • Every marking act is a button that says what it does, on the timeline or on the step it acts on. Right-click offers the same acts, and nothing is right-click only.
  • The top bar always says the next act of the Checklist.
  • A message reads in the timeline's own row and never covers the work.

What this sketch leaves out

  • Overview, Steps and Map are not drawn. Steps stays the full editor for flow and sub-processes, with the same step fields as here.
  • Dragging a band's side, trimming a video and typing a time work as they do today.
  • The buttons that only show a message here exist in the app already. Which keys Start transaction and Add note get is still open.
