Welcome to Process Discovery Tracker

Capture · Mine · Synthesize

This is the worker-side capture app. It runs in your system tray and records what you do (with markers and screenshots) so an analyst can later study the process.

Three hotkeys to remember:

  1. {{HK_START}} — Start a marker. Pops a label picker, then begins capturing under that name.
  2. {{HK_END}} — End the innermost open marker.
  3. {{HK_SHOT}} — Take a manual screenshot any time.

For guided walkthroughs (record + name + close in one panel), open the Live session window.

First time? Tell us who's recording — it's stamped onto every session so the analyst can tell sessions apart.

Reopen this from the tray → Help. Profile and Settings live at the top level of the tray menu.
