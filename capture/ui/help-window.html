How to record

  1. Start. {{START_HOW}} Recording starts with Transaction 1, and a small recording bar appears.
  2. The next transaction. {{TRANSACTION}} Each time you start the process again, {{NEXT_HOW}} The transaction before it ends by itself.
  3. Pause. Click Pause on the bar while you do something that is not part of the process, and Resume when you are back.
  4. Stop. Click Stop on the bar, then Stop recording. The summary shows where the recording is saved.
  5. Hand it over. In the summary, click Make a zip for the analyst and send the zip, or copy the recording folder.

What is recorded

The programs and windows you use, your clicks and typing, and screenshots. What you type into password fields is not recorded. Nothing is sent over the network: the recording stays on this computer until you hand it over.
