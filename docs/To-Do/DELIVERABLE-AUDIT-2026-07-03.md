# Deliverable audit -- the memo and the PDD (2026-07-03)

> **Read-only audit.** Every section of the findings memo and the Process
> Definition Document, judged against the **use gate**
> ([APPROACHABILITY-REDESIGN-2026-07-03](APPROACHABILITY-REDESIGN-2026-07-03.md) §3),
> the honesty rules the *screens* now obey
> ([DISCLOSURE-MODEL](../architecture/DISCLOSURE-MODEL.md)), and structural

> cut / rewrite.** The verdict column is a recommendation, not a change.
>
> **Method / scope of evidence.** Both documents were generated by the bundled
> Python from a re-analyzed copy of the committed demo session

> the PDD is the `--pdd-out` docx. Numbers quoted below were read back from
> those generated files and from `stats.json` / `analysis.json`.
>
> **Demo-data caveat (important).** The demo is a tiny, clean, 4-case synthetic
> session. Several verdicts below (percentile furniture, extrapolated rates,

> again an acceptance test" (§1). Where a verdict depends on N, it is marked
> **[N-limited]**. **The ideal-memo diff against a REAL session is out of scope
> (owner-gated).**

## Part 1 effect (verified)

The R3 export wiring (ADR-0028 §4, committed this session) makes analyst-written
work-item names appear in **both** documents. With a test `group_annotations`
entry on case `Order #10900` (name "Enter the order lines" + note; name "Re-key
order into SAP"; a waste flag on the Excel visit):

- **memo.md / memo.docx**, Case-by-case section, gains:
  `- **Order #10900: Enter the order lines** -- unit price is always 49.99 -- could default it`
  `- **Order #10900: Re-key order into SAP**`
- **PDD.docx** shows the same names **twice** -- once in §1 (the shared memo
  model) and once in §4.5 as work-item headings:
  §1: `Order #10900: Re-key order into SAP` · §4.5 heading: `Re-key order into SAP`
  with the note `unit price is always 49.99` and `Flags: Waste` flowing in.

Without any `group_annotations` (the real demo posture) both documents are
byte-identical to before -- the change is additive.

## Top gaps ranked (max 10, severity-ordered -- owner decides; no redesigns)

1. **ROI §5.4 prints a manufactured-zero table below its own "needs input"
   note.** The whole section should gate on entered volume + wage (one sentence
   + Edit-assumptions CTA); the zero-filled 5-column grid is the exact
   anti-pattern §4 named. *(honesty: manufactured zeros / gate-in-name-only)*
2. **Findings 3-5 (memo + PDD §1) duplicate the §5.3 automation-candidate
   table** -- one detector feed rendered as findings *and* as a ranked table.
   Pick one home. *(§2 L3 duplication)*
3. **Raw app/process tokens in business prose** -- "sapgui", "chrome", "excel"
   prefix the candidate/finding sequence labels (§5.3, findings 3-5); §6.1/§6.2
   print `Text entry -- app.exe`. Business prose should not carry
   `snake_case`/`.exe`/process tokens. *(honesty rule 4)*
4. **§6.1 "variations" are single-occurrence paths at small N** -- 2 freq-1 rows
   out of 4 cases presented with fact authority as "deviations." At real N this
   may be real; at demo N it is noise. *(use gate / N-limited)*
5. **Two overlapping time tables in §4.4** -- "Where the time went" and
   "Activity statistics" list the same 5 activities with overlapping columns.
   One earns residence. *(one-fact-one-place)*
6. **§4.4 Activity-statistics Median column is percentile furniture** -- median
   over 1-5 observations per activity says nothing yet. *(honesty: stat
   furniture at small N / N-limited)*
7. **Cycle-time number disagrees across the deliverable** -- memo "mean 46s"
   (wall-clock) vs PDD §4.4 "avg 45.3s each" (active). One deliverable, one
   metric name, two numbers/bases. *(one-source-of-truth leak)*
8. **Fact-strip cell "1 (9 repeated sequences)" merges two unrelated counts**
   (rekey=1, repeated-sequence=9) into a single "Values re-typed" cell.
   *(honesty: merged quantities)*
9. **Per-hour extrapolation at sub-6-minute N** -- "16 (168.4/h)" switches (fact
   strip + finding 2) projects a rate from a 5m 42s window. *(honesty:
   extrapolated furniture / N-limited)*
10. **§4.5 fragment rows still carry embedded case values when no visit is
    named** ("Salesforce: Search - CUST-10900", "Enter 29 in Quantity") -- the
    work-item *headings* are clean, but the fragment layer still shows the raw
    value-laden step names the R3 unit exists to escape. Expected fallback (a
    named session cures it); flagged so it is a known residual, not silent.
    *(use gate / by-design until analyst names visits)*

## Judgments limited by demo data (stated honestly)

- Gaps **4, 6, 9** (single-occurrence variants, median furniture, per-hour
  extrapolation) are **N-limited**: they are worst on a 4-case, 5m 42s
  synthetic session and would soften on a real 1-hour capture with genuine
  repetition. They still matter because the use gate is a real-session test --
  but the *severity ranking* above could shift once measured on real data.
- Gap **10** (value-laden fragment names) only shows because the demo has **no
  `group_annotations`**; the moment an analyst names the visits (the R3
  workflow) the headings carry business names. It is a fallback artifact, not a
  defect.
- The **ideal-memo diff against a real session** (does the memo say what an
  analyst would actually write after 45 minutes?) is **out of scope and
  owner-gated** -- it needs a real capture and the owner's sit-down, per the
  cycle's real-session acceptance rule.
- Not audited here: the embedded process-map image (no map was passed to this
  run) and the docx visual formatting (heading levels 3-5 nesting depth in
  §4.5 was verified present but not eyeballed for readability in Word).
